Hello,

I am a Post-doctoral fellow working on methods to study the metabolism of organism and microbial community in the MICROCOSME Team.

Metabolism is the set of biochemical reactions that allow the growth of an organism. Biochemical reactions are conversion of substance into other substance through an energy change. In metabolism, most of the reactions are catalysed (meaning increasing their rate of conversion) by specialised proteins called enzymes. To grow, an organism retrieves nutrients (substances) from their environment, degrades them to generate energy (process called catabolism) and uses this energy to produce other metabolties required for their growth (process called anabolism). To understand the metabolism of an organism, we need to find which metabolites can be used by it. One possibility is by identifying its enzymes. This is possible because enzymes (like other proteins) are generated by DNA transcription and translation.
